Kithul pani comes from one palm. A tapper climbs the palm and catches its sap in a clay pot.

How kithul pani is made

Three steps from the palm to the bottle, all done by hand.

  1. Collecting the sap

    The sap drips from the cut flower stalk into a clay pot tied to the palm.

  2. Boiling it down

    The sap boils in a wide pan over a wood fire and is stirred until it turns thick and dark.

  3. Pani ready to pour

    Cooled, it pours slowly from a spoon. Then it goes into bottles and is sealed.
